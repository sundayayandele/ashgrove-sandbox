# Lending policy

## Affordability
- Total monthly debt repayments, including the new loan, must stay at or below **40% of monthly income**.
- Applicants need at least **6 months** of credit history.
- Loans run from EUR 1,000 to EUR 25,000 over 12 to 60 months.

## Who decides
Every credit decision is made by a **named human loan officer**. Software may produce a recommendation with a rationale; it may never record a decision.

## Explaining decisions
Every decline must include a reason the applicant can understand. Applicants can ask for that reason at any time.

## Data we must not use
Decisions must not use nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, age or any proxy for them, including names.
