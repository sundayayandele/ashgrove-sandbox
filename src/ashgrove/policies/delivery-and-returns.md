# Delivery and returns

Standard delivery takes 2 to 7 working days in the Netherlands, Germany and the UK, and 5 to 12 working days in Nigeria and South Africa.

Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items.
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are shipped.
