# Refund policy

## Who can get a refund
Customers can ask for a refund within **30 days of delivery** for any delivered order.

## Who can approve it
- Support agents can issue refunds up to **EUR 100** per order.
- Refunds above EUR 100 need approval from a **support lead** before they are issued. The order system enforces this.
- Nobody issues refunds on orders that have not been delivered. Cancel the order instead.

## Damaged or wrong items
Refund the full item price when the customer reports damage or a wrong item within 30 days. Ask for a photo when the amount is above EUR 100.

## What we never do
- Refund more than the order value.
- Promise a refund before checking the order status.
