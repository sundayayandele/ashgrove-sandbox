# AI use policy

## Telling people
Customers must be told when they are talking to an AI assistant.

## What AI agents may do on their own
- Read data they need for the task.
- Draft replies, notes and recommendations.
- Issue refunds up to EUR 100.

## What always needs a person
- Refunds above EUR 100.
- Any credit or hiring decision.
- Deleting data, resetting systems or changing permissions.

## Logging
Every agent action is traced and kept for 5 years. Agents act under their own identity, on behalf of a named employee where one is involved.
