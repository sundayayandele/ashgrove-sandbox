# Bereavement and hardship

## Retail orders
Ashgrove does **not** offer bereavement discounts or special fares on retail orders. Normal refund and return rules apply.
Staff may offer free express delivery as a goodwill gesture, up to EUR 15.

## Ashgrove Credit customers
Customers facing bereavement, illness or job loss can ask for a **payment pause of up to 3 months**. A loan officer must approve it.

## What agents must not do
Never promise a discount, refund or payment pause that this policy does not allow. If unsure, hand the case to a person.
