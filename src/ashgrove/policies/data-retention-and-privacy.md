# Data retention and privacy

## Erasure requests
When a customer asks us to delete their data, erase it in **every system within 30 days**: CRM, helpdesk, orders and lending.
Keep only what the law requires (for example, invoices for 7 years).

## Least data
Systems and AI agents read only the personal data a task needs. Contact details are read one customer at a time, never in bulk.

## Retention
- Support tickets: 2 years after closing.
- Applicant data: 6 months after the role is filled.
- Audit logs: 5 years.
