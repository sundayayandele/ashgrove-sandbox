# Hiring policy

- Screening uses only the job's published must-have and nice-to-have criteria.
- First-round screening is **name-blind**. Use the name_blind view in the HR system.
- Every move to the next stage or rejection is decided by a **named human recruiter**, with a written reason.
- Software may write screening notes only when each claim is backed by a quote from the CV.
- Applicant data is kept for 6 months after the role is filled, then deleted.
